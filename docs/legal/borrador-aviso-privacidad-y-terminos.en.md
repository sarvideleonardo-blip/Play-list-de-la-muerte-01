## Part 1. Privacy Notice

**Controller:** [name of the controller or project]
**Privacy contact email:** [email]
**Last updated:** [date]

### 1. Data we collect

- **Account data:** email address and sign-in method (for example, Google).
- **Playlist data:** the 30 songs you choose, taken from Spotify (title, artist and song identifier).
- **Legacy data:** the final message and personal fragments you write, if you decide to write them.
- **Display name (optional):** only if you decide to write one. You can leave it blank.
- **Trusted contacts (optional, but required for publication):** the names and emails of the people you designate.
- **Voice dictation:** if you use your browser's dictation, the audio may be processed by a browser or operating system service, outside our control. We recommend typing the text if you prefer not to share your voice.

**Sensitive data:** your messages may reveal sensitive information (health, beliefs, intimate life). We only collect it if you decide to write it, and we ask for your express consent when we save it.

### 2. What we use your data for

- Creating and maintaining your account and playlist.
- Sending you activity notices (see Part 3).
- Publishing your legacy only in the cases provided for in the Terms and the Annex.
- Keeping the service secure.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. There are no ads or paid versions at this stage.

### 3. Activity notices

We will send you emails to confirm that you are still here, following the process described in the Annex. You can pause the notices or withdraw your account at any time.

### 4. Who we share data with

- **Technical providers** that host the database, sign-in and email delivery, under confidentiality agreements.
- **Spotify**, to display and play the songs. This app is not affiliated with Spotify and is not endorsed by it.
- **Your trusted contacts**, only for the confirmation process described in the Annex, and only with what is necessary for that function.
- **The public**, only if your legacy is activated and published, under the terms you have chosen.

### 5. Publishing your legacy

When a legacy is published:

- Your profile picture, email and account are not shown.
- Your name appears only if you chose it. By default, publication is anonymous, with a random legacy number.
- **The text of your message may identify you** even if your name is not shown. We ask you to review it before publication.

### 6. Retention and deletion

- We keep your data while your account is active.
- You can withdraw your playlist or your account while you are alive. We delete your data within [30] days, except for what the law requires us to keep.
- Once published, the legacy may have been copied or archived by third parties beyond our control. We cannot guarantee its removal from third-party sites.

### 7. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, cancellation or objection to the processing of your data (ARCO rights) by writing to [email]. In accordance with Mexico's personal data protection law in force, we will respond within the time limits it sets. [Review with lawyer.]

### 8. Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will tell you by email and ask you to accept the new version before you keep using the service.

## Part 2. Terms of Use

### 1. What it is (and what it is not)

Playlist of Death is a tool for creating a list of songs and a personal message as a legacy. **It is not a will, it has no legal validity as a disposition of property, and it does not replace a will drawn up before a notary.** If you want to leave legal provisions, consult a notary.

### 2. Requirements

- Be 18 years old or older.
- Be the person who creates their own list. You cannot create lists for someone else.

### 3. Your content

- You keep the rights to your content.
- You give us limited permission to store it, show it to you and, if it is activated, show it publicly under these terms.
- You are responsible for what you write. Do not publish content that violates third-party rights.

### 4. Prohibited content

Messages or fragments are not allowed that:

- Contain third-party data (home addresses, account numbers, data about minors) without their consent.
- Accuse someone of crimes without basis, or threaten or harass people.
- Contain illegal material or material that exploits others.
- Promote violence or harm to oneself or others.

We may remove content that breaks these rules. There is a button to report third-party content.

### 5. Legacy activation

Activation follows the process in the Annex. **No legacy is published merely because time has passed, nor because of a code alone, nor without the confirmation of a trusted contact and the cancellation period.** While the account is active, publication does not happen.

### 6. Withdrawing a legacy

You can withdraw your legacy at any time while you are alive and have access to your account. After publication, withdrawal can be requested by a trusted contact or a direct relative, with documentation proving the family relationship or representation. **Prudent default (editable):** we review the request within [15] business days and, while it is under review, the content is hidden.

### 7. Limitations

The service is offered "as is." We do not guarantee permanent availability. Spotify may change its terms, which could affect song playback.

### 8. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Mexico. Any dispute will be submitted to the courts of [city]. [Review with lawyer.]

### 9. Acceptance

When you create your list, you tick the box: "I have read and accept the Privacy Notice and the Terms, including the activation process." Each trusted contact also accepts these terms when they are designated. If the terms change, we will ask you to accept the new version.

## Annex. Notice and activation process

This annex sets out the original proposal by Leonardo, with the prudent default values I propose for the open decisions. Everything is editable during review.

### A. Inactivity notice

| Moment | What happens |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | Last account activity (sign-in, edit, or clicking "I'm still here"). |
| Month 6 without activity | "Are you still here?" email with the "I'm still here" button. Any activity restarts the period. |
| Month 6 + 30 days | First reminder. |
| Month 6 + 60 days | Second reminder. |
| Month 6 + 90 days | Third reminder. If there are trusted contacts, they are told the process could start. |

### B. Contact confirmation

After the third reminder with no response:

1. **One** trusted contact is asked to confirm by email that the person has died, using a form and a statement that they are doing so in good faith.
2. The other contact, if there is one, is notified so they can object.
3. **Prudent default (editable):** without a designated trusted contact, the legacy **stays private and is not published**. The app does not start the process.

### C. Cancellation period (30 days)

After the contact confirms:

- An email notice is sent to the owner and to all registered contacts.
- **Prudent default (editable):** any of the contacts, or the owner if they log back in, can cancel the process during the 30 days. Cancelling returns the account to active status.
- If no one cancels, the legacy is published.

### D. Publication

- The playlist is published, and the message only if the person chose that.
- Both are anonymous by default, and the name appears only if the person chose it while alive.

### E. Activation code

**Prudent default (editable): the 8-digit code no longer activates anything on its own.** It stays as part of the process: the contact enters it as proof that they have the relationship, but publication still requires steps B and C. The code is stored encrypted.

### F. Number of contacts

**Prudent default (editable): two contacts are recommended.** One is enough to start the confirmation, but both receive all notices.
