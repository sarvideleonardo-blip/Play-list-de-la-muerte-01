# Contributing to Playlist of Death

Thanks for wanting to help. This project deals with a sensitive subject: people's memories and their privacy. That's why we're looking for people in programming, design and privacy, and also people with experience in grief, digital legacy or law.

## How you can help

- **Review the draft privacy notice and terms** in [docs/legal](docs/legal/borrador-aviso-privacidad-y-terminos.en.md): what's clear, what's missing, and what risks you see.
- **Improve the app:** validate song links, split `src/App.tsx` into smaller pieces, and write tests for the access rules.
- **Design and accessibility:** text, contrast, keyboard use and screen readers.
- **Translations and writing** for the app's texts.
- **Ideas:** open an issue to propose something new or discuss an existing change.

## Basic rules

- Don't share real personal data, real messages or real people's songs in issues, pull requests or screenshots.
- Decisions about consent, activation and publication are made by the project owner. You can propose alternatives and argue for them.
- Keep it respectful and use clear language. The subject is sensitive.

## Getting started

1. Look at the issues labeled `good first issue`.
2. Fork the repo, create a branch and open a pull request explaining what you changed and why.
3. If your change affects the database or privacy, describe it in the pull request.
